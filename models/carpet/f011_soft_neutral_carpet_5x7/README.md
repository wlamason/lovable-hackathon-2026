# F011 Soft Neutral Carpet 5x7

Scandinavian living-room area rug, 60 × 84 × 0.5 in, modeled as a thin pile slab with a lightly rounded edge, an oatmeal field, and a warm stone border on its own material slot. Origin sits at the bottom center of the footprint, front facing -Y. Rebuild from this folder with Blender 4.2: `blender -b --factory-startup --python build.py -- --outdir .`
