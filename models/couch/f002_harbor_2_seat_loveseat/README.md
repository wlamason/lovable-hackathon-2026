# F002 Harbor 2-Seat Loveseat

Coastal living-room loveseat (60 in wide × 35 in deep × 34 in tall) with a sand-linen track-arm body, two loose seat cushions, two leaned back cushions, tapered driftwood legs, and navy-striped throw pillows. Rebuild from an empty scene with Blender 4.2: `blender -b --factory-startup --python build.py -- --outdir .` (writes `model.glb`, `model.blend`, `preview.png`, and `metadata.json` next to the script).
