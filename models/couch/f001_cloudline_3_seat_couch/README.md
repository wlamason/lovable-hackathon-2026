F001 Cloudline 3-Seat Couch is a modern living-room sofa, 84 in wide by 36 in deep by 33 in tall, with a low plinth, track arms, three seat cushions, three back cushions, short legs, and two throw pillows. Rebuild it with Blender 4.2 from this folder: `blender -b --factory-startup --python build.py -- --outdir .` The script writes `model.glb`, `model.blend`, `preview.png`, and `metadata.json`.
